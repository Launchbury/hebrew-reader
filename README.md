# Classic Fables Hebrew Reader: Audio

These are the audio recordings of the five stories in *Classic Fables Hebrew Reader*, by John Launchbury, available on [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Classic-Fables-Hebrew-Reader/dp/B0HCF5FSD6). Follow along in the book, or listen first and then read.

**Listen online: https://launchbury.github.io/hebrew-reader/**

## The stories

1. The Three Billy Goats (שלושת התישים)
2. The Tortoise and the Hare (הצב והארנב)
3. Stone Soup (מרק אבנים)
4. The North Wind and the Sun (הרוח הצפונית והשמש)
5. The Ugly Duckling (הברווזון המכוער)

Each story can be played in the browser or downloaded as an MP3 from the page above. The MP3 files are also in the [`audio`](audio) folder of this repository.
